Internal reliability

We calculate internal reliability with McDonald’s \(\omega\), which is considered acceptable when above .7. Thus this shows good internal reliability

[1] 0.9398467

Confirmatory factor analysis (CFA)

For the CFA, I will keep in mind the following criteria:

  • CFI > .9
  • TLI > .9
  • RMSEA < .8
  • SRMR < .8

One factor model

Firstly, I will carry a one factor model with the additional correlation that we established in the Introduction (Residual correlation between AI_1_3 and AI_1_7; and between AI_2_2 and AI_2_4).

Item Responce Theory (IRT)

I carry out two distinctive IRT. One for the entire scale, the other with the two subfactors (interest_subjective and interest_behaviour) divided.

One factor model

This graph represent how informative on average the items are in relation to the ability of the participants (\(\theta\)). The dotted line represents the mean \(\theta\).

We can notice that it is in general pretty normally distributed around 0, with a little skewness into the lower values. This means that it is more informative for the people with lower ability. I think it is interesting that is focused that the majority of distribution is below the mean.

We carried out analysis for the individual items.

For each item, \(a\) represents the discrimination parameter, i.e. how much the value vary in relation to the ability \(\theta\). A high \(a\) means that the probability of answering an item correctly changes very sharply as \(\theta\) increases.

  • AI_1_1 : I enjoyed visiting art classes as a subject in school
  • AI_1_2 : I enjoy talking about art with others
  • AI_1_3 : I have many friends/acquaintances who are interested in art
  • AI_1_4 : I am interested in art
  • AI_1_5 : I am always looking for new artistic impressions and experiences
  • AI_1_6 : During my everyday life I spontaneously notice art objects that I find fascinating
  • AI_1_7 : I come from a family that is interested in art

Answers from 1 (less than once per year) to 7 (once per week or more often)

  • AI_2_1 : How often do you visit art museums or art galleries on average?
  • AI_2_2 : How often do you read books, magazines or catalogues about art?
  • AI_2_3 : How often do you view images of artworks (picture books, internet, etc.)?
